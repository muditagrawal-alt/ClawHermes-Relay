# AGENTS.md — OpenClaw Slack Collaboration Guidelines

- Channel policy: Only collaborate in the designated Slack channel with `@mention` gating active.
- Always reply inside the originating Slack thread (`thread_ts`) so human requests, OpenClaw delegations, Hermes research replies, and OpenClaw executive summaries stay cleanly threaded.
- Keep handoff tags (`[handoff:...]`) intact across delegation and final summary so Arize Phoenix traces correlate OpenClaw and Hermes runs.
- Keep Slack messages formatted cleanly for Slack (`*bold*`, bullet lists, compact tables/blocks; avoid giant walls of text).
